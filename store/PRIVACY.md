# Privacy draft

Not a published policy. Matthew still has to approve the wording and host it.

The Waking keeps a save on this device: chapter, position, settings, endings, and which choices this device already picked.

If a public counter is connected later, a choice sends only the chapter, the question id, and the option label. No account. No name.

If analytics is connected later, a chapter start, a chapter complete, a drop off, or a crash sends the event name, the chapter id, and a segment number. No free text from the player.

Book trailers play from files on this site. They do not contact an ad network. AdMob is not connected. Amazon and the author hub open only if the player chooses those links after a trailer.

Backup codes are a copy of the save slot. They stay on the device until the player copies them.
